Your instrument

The chord says what the band plays. It does not say what a bass player plays: in C/E the chord is C, and the bass is on E. JamPilot measures the bass separately – it is not guessed from the chord.

Fretboard

Bass and guitar mode show a fretboard top-left. If you know your way around the neck, turn it off – the big display gets the full screen back.

Control guitar

A quiet, dry guitar pluck at every detected chord change. It is deliberately simple: wrong roots and major/minor thirds become audible immediately without turning JamPilot into an accompaniment.

Chord spelling

The same key is called A♯ or B♭, depending on the key of the song. JamPilot detects the key and spells accordingly – or you decide.
